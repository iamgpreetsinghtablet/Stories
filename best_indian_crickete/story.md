# The Little Master's Big Dream

![Cover](images/cover.jpg)

## Page 1

![Page 1](images/page_1.jpg)

Once upon a time, in a small town in India, there lived a young boy named Rohan. He was a huge fan of cricket and his idol was the legendary Indian cricketer, Sachin Tendulkar. Rohan spent most of his days playing cricket with his friends in the local park.

## Page 2

![Page 2](images/page_2.jpg)

Rohan's room was filled with posters of Sachin Tendulkar and he would often watch videos of his matches to learn new skills. He was determined to become the best cricketer in India, just like his idol.

## Page 3

![Page 3](images/page_3.jpg)

One day, Rohan's coach, Mr. Kumar, announced that the school team would be playing a match against a team from another town. Rohan was excited to play his first big match and he practiced day and night to prepare himself.

## Page 4

![Page 4](images/page_4.jpg)

The day of the match arrived and Rohan's team was ready to play. Rohan was nervous but excited as he put on his cricket gear and took the field. The opposing team was strong, but Rohan was determined to play his best game.

## Page 5

![Page 5](images/page_5.jpg)

The match began and Rohan played an amazing game. He scored a century and took several wickets, helping his team win the match. The crowd cheered and Rohan's teammates lifted him on their shoulders, celebrating their victory.

## Page 6

![Page 6](images/page_6.jpg)

After the match, Rohan's coach, Mr. Kumar, came up to him and said, 'Rohan, you have the potential to become one of the best cricketers in India. Keep practicing and never give up on your dreams.' Rohan was overjoyed and thanked his coach for his guidance and support.

## Page 7

![Page 7](images/page_7.jpg)

Rohan continued to work hard and soon became one of the best cricketers in his town. He was selected to play for the state team and eventually, he played for the Indian national team. Rohan's dream had come true and he was living his passion.

## Page 8

![Page 8](images/page_8.jpg)

Rohan's story is an inspiration to all the young cricketers out there, who dream of playing for their country one day. With hard work, determination, and passion, anything is possible. Rohan's journey is a testament to the fact that if you chase your dreams, you can achieve anything you set your mind to.
